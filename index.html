    Student Cards & Anti-Cheating System Active

    Each card has an individually assigned secret side to prevent students copying each other.

    🛡️ How the Anti-Cheating Secret Side System Works:

    The teacher and students stand opposite each other. The teacher whispers or signals each student their secret number: 1 = Top ⬆️, 2 = Right ➡️, 3 = Left ⬅️, 4 = Bottom ⬇️. The student holds the paper facing the camera and directs their chosen answer to that side:
    • 1: Top ⬆️ = Top edge pointing towards ceiling.
    • 2: Right ➡️ = Directed towards student's right hand.
    • 3: Left ⬅️ = Directed towards student's left hand.
    • 4: Bottom ⬇️ = Bottom edge pointing towards floor.

    1: Top ⬆️ (Ceiling)
    2: Right ➡️ (Student Right)
    3: Left ⬅️ (Student Left)
    4: Bottom ⬇️ (Floor)
